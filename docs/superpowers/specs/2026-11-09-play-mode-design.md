Add a Playing workspace using the selected board artwork, with human color and match length choices. GNUbg is authoritative for legal plays, dice, computer checker and cube decisions, scoring and Crawford. Keep hover/focus destination dots, complete legal sequences, per-step uncommitted undo and explicit turn confirmation by clicking either displayed die after the final legal checker step. Direction is persisted as right (default) or left, relative to the human player's home board at the bottom of the screen. Reflect geometry and hit targets together, keep numbers/text readable, and fix viewpoint to the human rather than flipping every turn. Both built-in and archive themes must work. Position IDs and engine rules remain independent of orientation.

Persist the native per-side results, any tied attempts, higher-die starter, and exact checkpoint. Pause at the result before either a human or bot checker move. On acknowledgment the starter uses precisely those two dice, without an ordinary roll or cube choice. Reloading either pending or result stage cannot consume randomness or auto-play the first turn.
