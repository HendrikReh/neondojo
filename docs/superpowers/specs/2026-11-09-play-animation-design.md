# Visible dice and checker play

Animate all actual dice rolls and checker moves in Playing, including opening rolls/ties, the bot opening turn, normal human and bot turns, hits, bar entry and bearing off. Show the bot's actual dice before its sequential checker moves; the opening winner consumes the existing opening dice without another roll. A single source click still moves one checker; rapid source clicks may queue legal steps. Turn confirmation, undo/reset and engine actions wait for pending animation; human checker intent is kept in order.

The engine remains authoritative. A successful action is persisted natively before local playback, and only after presentation finishes does the client accept the final decision state/open the next Cube/Roll prompt. Replay newly appended native records, silently applying already-previewed human moves before bot playback. Never replay saved history on load. Reload during playback restores the final durable native checkpoint; hiding the workspace cancels presentation and accepts that result. Audio accompanies each visible roll/move rather than playing the entire bot sequence at once.

Render moving checkers within the existing SVG using native board coordinates and the active theme artwork. Existing direction transforms apply to the overlay. Show captured checkers travelling to the bar. Use roughly 300ms per checker, rolling dice plus a short settled hold; reduced-motion uses brief fades. Animate undo/reset as reversed local steps. Cosmetic rolling faces never enter rule state or consume RNG. Test pure replay, movement geometry, interaction sequencing, opening bot dice/motion, sound timing, hidden/reload cancellation and real GNUbg matches.
